## 2. Team Speaking Roles (4-Person Breakdown)

### Speaker 1: Problem Statement & Market Opportunity
- **Focus**: The cost, time wastage, and security flaws in existing attendance systems.
- **Talking Points**:
  - Paper attendance takes 10–15% of instructional class time and is prone to human error.
  - Biometric fingerprint systems have slow read rates, raise hygiene concerns, and cost $150–$300 per unit.
  - Existing RFID readers lack visual feedback, require tedious manual USB data downloads, or force schools into proprietary vendor software.

### Speaker 2: Hardware Architecture & Python-First Firmware
- **Focus**: ESP32-S3 MicroPython firmware, ST7789 Color TFT, and the Onboarding Web Portal.
- **Talking Points**:
  - **100% Python-First Ecosystem**: The firmware runs pure **MicroPython** on the ESP32-S3, unifying hardware and backend into a single readable language.
  - **Modern Card-Based UI**: Dynamic light-themed screens featuring a clean "TAP YOUR ID CARD" home screen and a prominent "Present!" checkmark badge with Student Name and Enroll ID.
  - **Zero-Tool 1-Click Python Flasher (`flash_terminal.py`)**: Automatic driver detection, automated MicroPython installation, and instant code synchronization without IDE complexity.
  - **Captive Onboarding Hotspot**: If unconfigured, the device broadcasts `Smart-Attendance-Setup` at `http://192.168.4.1` with a live 30+ network Wi-Fi scanner dropdown (WLED-style).

### Speaker 3: Database & Anti-Tamper Security Architecture
- **Focus**: Supabase PostgreSQL, `enroll_id` tracking, Row Level Security (RLS), and atomic transactions.
- **Talking Points**:
  - The terminal only possesses a public `anon` key restricted strictly to executing our atomic `record_daily_attendance` function.
  - **Server-Authoritative Clock**: Attendance timestamps are generated by PostgreSQL on the server (`CURRENT_DATE`, `now()`), preventing students from hacking device clocks.
  - Database-enforced `UNIQUE (student_id, date)` constraint guarantees that no student can be marked present twice on the same day, even during rapid network retries.
  - Standardized schema: Student records indexed by **`enroll_id`** with instant status classification (`MARKED`, `ALREADY_MARKED`, `NO_DATA_FOUND`).

### Speaker 4: Safe Live Sync, Anti-Proxy Measures & Demo
- **Focus**: Live Google Sheets sync, buzzer audio verification, proxy defense, and live demo.
- **Talking Points**:
  - **Audio-Visual Proof**: Hardware piezo buzzer emits a crisp double-chirp (`Beep-Beep`) for "Present!", a long warning tone for "Already Marked", and 3 rapid clicks for "No Data Found", giving teachers instant audio confirmation from anywhere in the classroom.
  - **Anti-Proxy 2-Second Cooldown**: Reader enforces a strict 2-second rate-limiter, blocking rapid consecutive proxy card swipes.
  - **Read-Only Safety Shield**: Teachers only access a live Google Sheet. They cannot accidentally delete or break database records. Even if the entire spreadsheet is wiped, our `--resync` engine rebuilds it from Supabase in 1 second.
  - Executes the live scan on stage.

## 3. Proxy Prevention Strategies (How We Solve It)

Proxy attendance (one student scanning multiple friends' cards) is a common challenge in RFID deployments. Here is how our architecture addresses it:

1. **Anti-Rapid-Scan Cooldown (Rate Limiting)**:
   - The firmware enforces a 3-second lockout between scans. If multiple cards are swiped back-to-back within 2 seconds, the terminal flags them and sounds an alert.
2. **High-Visibility Visual Callout**:
   - The ST7789 display renders a large color-coded card with the student's **Full Name**, **Roll Number**, and **Class Section**. When mounted at the classroom door or teacher's desk, nearby staff or monitors immediately spot if a student scans multiple times or scans a card belonging to someone else.
3. **Dynamic Entry Window**:
   - Attendance is restricted to the first 10 minutes of a scheduled class period. Scans after the cutoff are automatically categorized as `LATE` or rejected.
4. **Camera Extension (Future Scope)**:
   - A companion ESP32-CAM module mounted adjacent to the terminal can snap a timestamped photo on every tap and upload it to Supabase Storage.

## 5. Live Demonstration Choreography

1. **Step 1: Setup Demo**:
   - Hold the BOOT button on the ESP32-S3 for 3 seconds.
   - Show the TFT screen displaying the **QR code** and "Smart-Attendance-Setup".
   - Scan the QR code with your phone camera -> show the light-themed web portal -> save settings.
2. **Step 2: Live Tap Demo**:
   - Have the Google Sheet open on a projector screen or laptop.
   - Scan a student card (or type `STU101` in the Serial Monitor).
   - Point to the TFT screen turning vibrant **Green** with the student's name.
   - Point to the projector screen: **the row appears in Google Sheets in under 2 seconds!**
3. **Step 3: Duplicate Scan Rejection**:
   - Scan the same card immediately.
   - The TFT screen turns **Yellow** ("ALREADY MARKED TODAY").
   - Google Sheet does not add a duplicate row.
4. **Step 4: Accidental Deletion Recovery**:
   - On the Google Sheet, highlight all rows and hit **Delete** (demonstrating an accidental teacher mistake).
   - Run `python gsheet_sync_worker.py --resync` in the terminal.
   - All rows are restored from Supabase into Google Sheets instantly.
